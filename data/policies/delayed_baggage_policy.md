
# Northstar Air — Delayed Baggage Policy

Policy ID: BAG-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-01
Status: Active

## 1. Reporting Delayed Baggage

Passengers should report delayed checked baggage within 24 hours of discovering that their baggage has not arrived.

A baggage report must include the passenger's booking reference and baggage tag number.

## 2. Essential Purchases

Passengers with eligible delayed-baggage incidents may request reimbursement for reasonable essential purchases.

The maximum reimbursement under this fictional Northstar Air policy is $150 per eligible incident.

Passengers must provide receipts for all claimed purchases.

## 3. Compensation Requirements

A compensation request requires:

- A valid booking reference.
- An existing delayed-baggage report.
- Receipts for claimed essential purchases.
- Verification that the incident qualifies under this policy.

## 4. Approval

ResolveAI may assess eligibility and recommend reimbursement.

ResolveAI must not approve, issue, or promise reimbursement.

A human support agent must approve reimbursement requests.

## 5. Baggage Tracking

Current baggage location and delivery status must be obtained from Northstar Air's baggage-tracking records.

The policy document does not contain real-time baggage location information.
