# Northstar Air Baggage Policy

## Reporting
Passengers must report delayed baggage within 24 hours.

## Reimbursement
Essential purchases may be reimbursed up to $150.
Receipts are required for reimbursement.

## Human Approval
A human agent must approve reimbursement requests.

## Baggage Tracking
Passengers can request their baggage status.
Real-time baggage location is not available.
